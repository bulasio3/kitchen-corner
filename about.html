About us

Good food, made fresh, brought to you.

Our story

Cavendish Kitchen Corner began as a small kitchen with one big idea: hot, tasty meals should be easy to order and quick to arrive.

Every dish is cooked to order from fresh ingredients, packed carefully, and delivered by our own riders so it reaches you at its best.

What we stand for

  • Freshness: nothing is cooked until you order it
  • Speed: delivery within 45 minutes
  • Fair prices: no hidden charges
  • Choice: meat and vegetarian meals every day
